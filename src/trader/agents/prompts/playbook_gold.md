# Instrument playbook: Gold (XAUUSD) — version 1
Drivers: real yields and the US dollar, Fed expectations, geopolitical stress and safe-haven flows, central-bank buying, inflation data.
Sessions: active from London to New York; spikes at US data releases and at the London fix.
Cautions: sharp moves on geopolitical headlines that often retrace; spreads widen outside London/NY; do not infer direction from one headline.
