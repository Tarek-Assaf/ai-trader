# Instrument playbook: NAS100 (US100) — version 1
Drivers: interest rate expectations and Fed communications, US CPI/jobs/PCE releases, mega-cap earnings (Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Tesla), risk appetite (VIX, yields), index rebalances.
Sessions: most liquidity from the US cash open (13:30 UTC, shifts with daylight saving); first 15 minutes after the open and release times are high-noise.
Cautions: wide spreads in the Asian session and around the daily break; gaps after earnings; trends can persist after high-impact data, but the first minutes are unreliable.
